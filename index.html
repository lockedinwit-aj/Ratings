Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know Before Ordering

How Do I Place A Custom Order?

Fill Out Our Custom Order Form On The Website. Your Messaging App Will Open With Your Request Ready To Send To (913) 291-8663.

Can I Request A Specific Team Or Organization?

Yes! We Create Custom Welcome Signs For Sports Teams, Fraternities, Sororities, And Other Personalized Designs.

Can I Send A Reference Picture?

Yes! You Can Attach An Inspiration Photo When Texting Us Your Custom Order Request.

How Much Do Custom Signs Cost?

Pricing Depends On The Design And Customization. Text Us Your Idea To Request A Quote.

How Long Does An Order Take?

Turnaround Time Depends On The Design And Current Order Volume. We'll Provide An Estimate When Discussing Your Order. (Typically 3-4 Weeks After Sending Deposit)

Do You Offer Pickup Or Shipping?

Yes! We Offer Both Pickup And Shipping For Our Custom Welcome Signs. Customers Are Responsible For Covering All Shipping Costs.
